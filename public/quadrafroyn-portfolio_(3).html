Questions

FREQUENTLY ASKED

Everything you wanted to know about partnering with Quadrafroyn.

For most engagements, we assemble a vetted team of 3–6 engineers within 7–10 business days. For urgent MVPs, we can mobilize a 2-engineer pod in 72 hours from the signed SOW. Our bench includes certified Java, React, Node.js, and mobile specialists.

We offer three models: (1) Fixed-bid projects with clear scope and milestones; (2) Dedicated offshore teams on monthly retainer; (3) Time & material for evolving scopes. Most clients start with fixed-bid for MVP, then transition to dedicated team once trust is established.

All work-for-hire IP transfers to you on payment. We sign NDAs before any technical discussion, and our engineers work on isolated VDI environments with role-based access. Code lives in your Git repositories from day one — we never lock you in.

Every engagement includes 30 days of free bug-fix support post-launch. Beyond that, we offer tiered SLAs (Standard, Business, Enterprise) with response times from 8 hours down to 30 minutes, plus optional embedded SRE coverage for mission-critical workloads.
